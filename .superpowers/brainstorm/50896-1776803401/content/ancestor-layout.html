Q3 — How should archived-ancestor boxes look in the Timeline?

Goal: visually show "this merge-result topic was formed by these ancestors which lived across these calls". Two layout options below — scroll to compare.

A

Ghost rows beneath the merge-result row

Each archived ancestor becomes its own faded, thin row directly under the merge-result row. The row shows a colored box spanning only the columns the ancestor was active (first-raised call → merge call). Compact; one row per ancestor.

↗ arrow at end of ghost bar points to the merge call where it folded in.
B

Indented, tree-nested ancestor rows with a flow connector

Archived ancestors render as regular cell-by-cell rows (like today), but indented and visually attached to the merge-result row via a tree connector (├ └). Their cells past the merge call show a "flows into ↗" connector.
